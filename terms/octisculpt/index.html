OctiSculpt is an iPad sculpting app by EvaMariaDreams. Your use of the app is subject to the Apple Standard End User License Agreement and applicable law.

Premium subscription

OctiSculpt offers an optional auto-renewing annual subscription. While active, Premium unlocks all brushes and materials and allows model and rendered image exports without rewarded advertisements. The App Store displays the applicable price and billing period before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple manages billing and refunds.

Rewarded advertisements

Watching an optional rewarded advertisement can unlock additional brushes and materials for 10 minutes after a verified reward. Exports may require separate rewarded advertisements unless Premium is active. Advertisements may not always be available.

Restoring purchases
