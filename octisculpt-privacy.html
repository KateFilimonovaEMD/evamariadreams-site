Privacy Policy

How OctiSculpt handles your creative projects, optional rewarded advertisements, and privacy choices.

Last updated: November 7, 2026
At a glance. OctiSculpt is an iPad sculpting app. Your sculptures and project files are stored locally on your device. We use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded ads, and advertising-related information may be processed by Google. You can manage applicable privacy choices in the consent interface and device settings.

1. Who we are

OctiSculpt is provided by EvaMariaDreams, an independent app studio based in Spain (referred to here as "we", "our", or "us"). For questions about personal data, contact privacy@evamariadreams.com. For product support, contact support@evamariadreams.com.

2. Projects and information stored on your device

OctiSculpt lets you create, edit, save, import, and export 3D content. Project data, project names, model geometry, materials, thumbnails, app settings, and related working files are stored locally on your device. OctiSculpt does not currently offer a developer-operated account or cloud project synchronization service.

When you export or share a file, you choose the destination. Files may then be handled by Apple Files, iCloud Drive, another app, or another service according to your choices and the relevant provider's privacy practices. Device backups may also be managed by Apple according to your device settings. We do not automatically upload your sculpture content to our servers.

3. Optional rewarded advertisements

OctiSculpt integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide optional non-personalized rewarded advertisements for certain features. OctiSculpt configures Google Mobile Ads requests to disable ad personalization and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. We also use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising-related privacy choices where required.

When advertising functionality is used, Google and its advertising partners may process data such as device and app information, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app or other identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent choices. The precise categories and purposes depend on device settings, region, Google technology, partner configuration, and your choices.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your advertising and privacy choices

In regions where consent is required, OctiSculpt uses a consent message to let you make advertising-related choices. Where required by the consent framework, you can revisit those choices through the app's privacy options. You can also manage relevant privacy settings in iPadOS. OctiSculpt does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized advertising may still involve limited data processing for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and reporting.

Consent preferences can affect the type or availability of advertising. Declining consent does not delete your locally stored projects. Our app checks advertising eligibility before requesting an ad; an optional reward may be unavailable if an ad cannot be shown.

5. Why data may be processed

Locally stored project data is used to provide the sculpting and document-management features you request. Advertising-related processing supports optional ads, consent management, fraud prevention, measurement, and the operation of advertising services. Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential advertising purposes, that processing is subject to your consent choices. Some limited processing may be necessary for legal compliance, security, or legitimate interests, as permitted by applicable law.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your sculpting projects. Advertising-related data may be shared with or processed by Google and advertising partners through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in accordance with applicable configurations, user choices, and their policies. Files you deliberately export or share are handled by the destinations you choose.

7. Retention and deletion

Project and preference files stored in the app remain on your device until you delete the relevant data, remove the app, or manage the data using iPadOS file and backup tools. Removing the app may delete local app data, but copies you exported or backed up may remain in their respective locations.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention policies. If you email us, we use your message and contact details to respond and retain them only as reasonably needed for support, follow-up, or legal obligations.

8. International data processing

Advertising partners, including Google, may process data outside your country or the European Economic Area. The relevant providers are responsible for using applicable international data-transfer safeguards where required by law. See their published policies for further details.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning personal data we control, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, email privacy@evamariadreams.com.

For advertising information held directly by Google, you can use Google's privacy tools or contact Google. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also complain to your competent data protection authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) where appropriate.

10. Children

OctiSculpt does not request a user account or personal profile to create 3D models. Advertising availability and any age-related restrictions should follow the app's published age rating, relevant platform rules, and applicable law. If you believe personal information about a child has been provided to us through a support request, please contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version at this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
