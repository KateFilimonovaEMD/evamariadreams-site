Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Caribbean Sea Journey, including beta versions distributed through Apple TestFlight.

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Overview

EvaMariaDreams respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Caribbean Sea Journey.

Caribbean Sea Journey is currently a work-in-progress game and may be distributed to a limited group of external testers through Apple TestFlight.

Player profiles

Caribbean Sea Journey automatically creates a game profile when the game connects to Microsoft PlayFab.

The game generates a random identifier and stores it locally on your device. This identifier is sent to PlayFab so that the game can create and access your player profile.

This identifier is not created from your name, email address, Apple ID, advertising identifier, contact information, or other information stored on your device.

PlayFab may also assign an internal identifier to the player profile.

Player display name

Caribbean Sea Journey allows you to enter a player display name.

The display name you provide is transmitted to and stored through Microsoft PlayFab. It is associated with your game profile and is used to provide profile-related functionality within the game.

You are not required to enter your legal or real name. You should not include your email address, phone number, home address, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in your display name.

The game may also store the number of times the display name has been changed.

Information stored through PlayFab

Depending on the functionality used in the game, PlayFab may process and store information such as:

  • a randomly generated player identifier;
  • an internal PlayFab identifier;
  • the player display name;
  • the number of display-name changes;
  • game profile information;
  • technical information required to operate the service.

This information is used to create and maintain the game profile, save and retrieve profile information, and support the technical operation of the game.

Crash and diagnostic information

Caribbean Sea Journey uses Sentry to identify crashes, software errors, and technical problems.

When an error occurs, Sentry may receive technical and diagnostic information such as:

  • error messages;
  • stack traces;
  • application version;
  • operating system and platform information;
  • device type and technical characteristics;
  • timestamps;
  • diagnostic logs associated with the error.

Sentry is configured not to intentionally send the player display name, PlayFab identifier, screenshots, interface hierarchy, or default personally identifiable information.

Diagnostic information is used only to investigate errors, improve stability, and maintain the game.

Local data

Caribbean Sea Journey stores certain information locally on your device, including the randomly generated identifier used to access the PlayFab profile and other game settings or progress.

Local information may be removed when you delete the game, clear its local data, or reset your device.

Deleting the game from your device may not automatically delete information already stored through PlayFab or Sentry.

How information is used

Information handled in connection with Caribbean Sea Journey may be used to:

  • create and maintain player profiles;
  • save and retrieve the player display name;
  • provide game functionality;
  • diagnose crashes and technical errors;
  • improve game stability and performance;
  • prevent misuse or unauthorized activity;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services

Caribbean Sea Journey uses third-party service providers that process information on behalf of EvaMariaDreams.

Microsoft PlayFab

Microsoft PlayFab provides player authentication, profile storage, and backend functionality.

PlayFab may process the randomly generated player identifier, internal PlayFab identifier, player display name, display-name change information, and technical information required to provide its services.

Information processed by PlayFab is also subject to Microsoft’s applicable privacy terms.

Sentry

Sentry provides crash reporting and error diagnostics.

Sentry may process technical and diagnostic information required to identify, investigate, and resolve software errors.

Information processed by Sentry is also subject to Sentry’s applicable privacy terms.

Advertising and tracking

Caribbean Sea Journey does not currently contain third-party advertising.

EvaMariaDreams does not sell personal information.

Information collected through the game is not used to track players across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.

Legal basis for processing

Where applicable under data-protection law, information is processed because it is necessary to provide the game and its requested functionality.

Diagnostic information may also be processed for the legitimate interests of maintaining security, investigating software errors, preventing misuse, and improving the stability of the game.

Where consent is required by applicable law, information will be processed on the basis of that consent.

Data retention

Player profile information stored through PlayFab may be retained while the player profile remains active and for a reasonable period afterward, unless deletion is requested or longer retention is required by law.

Crash and diagnostic information stored through Sentry may be retained for a limited period according to the retention settings used by EvaMariaDreams and Sentry.

Support and privacy communications may be retained for a reasonable period for communication, recordkeeping, security, and legal purposes.

Access and deletion requests

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with your player profile.

Because Caribbean Sea Journey does not require an email address to create a player profile, EvaMariaDreams may need information from the game or device to locate the correct PlayFab profile.

To submit a request, contact privacy@evamariadreams.com .

Please include:

  • the name Caribbean Sea Journey;
  • your player display name, if one was entered;
  • any player identifier provided inside the game or by EvaMariaDreams support;
  • a description of your request.

EvaMariaDreams may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify that the request relates to the correct player profile.

Children’s privacy

Caribbean Sea Journey is not intended to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

Players should not enter a real name, email address, phone number, home address, or other sensitive personal information as their display name.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact privacy@evamariadreams.com .

International data processing

PlayFab and Sentry may process information on servers located outside the country in which you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.

Security

EvaMariaDreams uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled in connection with Caribbean Sea Journey.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

TestFlight and beta versions

Beta versions of Caribbean Sea Journey may be distributed through Apple TestFlight for testing and evaluation.

Beta software may contain incomplete features, errors, and diagnostic functionality. Information collected during beta testing is handled according to this Privacy Policy.

Apple may separately process information in connection with TestFlight under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Support communications

If you contact EvaMariaDreams by email, the information you provide in your message, including your email address and any details you choose to share, may be used to respond to your request and provide support.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Caribbean Sea Journey develops or its data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, data-access requests, or data-deletion requests, please contact EvaMariaDreams at privacy@evamariadreams.com .
