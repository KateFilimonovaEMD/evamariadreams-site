How the numbers get here

OctiMonitor is our own monitoring SDK for Metal apps, with a Unity version on the way. No third-party analytics and no identifiers.

1. MeasureEvery frame the app records frame time, CPU time and GPU time from Metal, without allocating memory.
2. SummarizeEvery 30 seconds it turns them into p50, p95 and p99, dropped frames, hitch time, memory and heat per material.
3. SendOne small anonymous report goes to the backend. The app's key can add reports and nothing else.
4. AggregateThis page reads only totals and averages through one read-only function, every 10 seconds.

p95 per material and over time is the frame-weighted mean of each 30-second window's p95. Hitch time counts only frames that missed their display slot: under 5 ms/s is smooth, 5–10 is noticeable, above 10 is stutter.
