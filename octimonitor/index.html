      How the numbers get here

      OctiMonitor is our own monitoring SDK for Metal apps, with a Unity version on the way. No third-party analytics and no identifiers.

      1MeasureEvery frame the app records when it reached the screen, its CPU time and its GPU time, straight from Metal.under 2 µs per frame
      2SummarizeFrames go into histograms, plus hitch time, dropped frames, memory and heat per scene.every 30 s
      3SendOne small anonymous report goes to the backend. The app's key can only add reports, within limits.no identifiers
      4AggregateOne read-only function merges every report's histograms, so percentiles cover all frames.live, every 10 s

      Percentiles are read from the merged histograms of every frame in view, accurate to 0.125 ms below 50 ms. Hitch time counts only frames that missed their display slot: under 5 ms/s is smooth, 5–10 is noticeable, above 10 is stutter. Full method
