      How the numbers get here

      OctiMonitor is our own monitoring SDK for Metal apps, with a Unity version on the way. No third-party analytics and no identifiers.

      1MeasureEvery frame the app records frame time, CPU time and GPU time straight from Metal.0 allocations per frame
      2SummarizeFrames become p50, p95 and p99, dropped frames, hitch time, memory and heat per scene.every 30 s
      3SendOne small anonymous report goes to the backend. The app's key can only add reports.no identifiers
      4AggregateThis page reads totals and averages through a single read-only function.live, every 10 s

      p95 per scene and over time is the frame-weighted mean of each 30-second window's p95. Hitch time counts only frames that missed their display slot: under 5 ms/s is smooth, 5–10 is noticeable, above 10 is stutter.
