About

Independent app studio.

EvaMariaDreams is an independent studio creating thoughtful digital products with a focus on clarity, calm interaction, and everyday usefulness. Octi Flow is part of that direction — a wellness-oriented app experience designed to support healthy routines in a simple and approachable way.

What we make

EvaMariaDreams develops consumer-facing digital experiences with an emphasis on elegant interfaces, focused functionality, and a warm visual identity. The goal is to create apps that feel light, clear, and supportive rather than overloaded or distracting.

About Octi Flow

Octi Flow is a hydration and wellness-oriented app designed to help users build consistent habits, personalize reminders, and unlock optional premium features for an expanded experience. The product is intended for general wellness and informational use.
