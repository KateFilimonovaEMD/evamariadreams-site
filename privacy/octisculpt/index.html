1. Who is responsible

EvaMariaDreams provides OctiSculpt. For questions about privacy, contact privacy@evamariadreams.com. Third-party services, including Google and Apple, may act as independent controllers for data they process under their own policies.

2. Projects, models, and preferences

OctiSculpt allows you to create and edit sculpting projects, models, materials, and app preferences. These are designed to be handled on your device as part of the App's core functionality. We do not operate a user-account or cloud-project hosting service for OctiSculpt. We do not intentionally upload your sculpting projects to our servers.

When you explicitly choose to export or share a file, the destination you select, such as another app, cloud storage provider, or recipient, may process it under its own privacy practices. Device backups and syncing managed by Apple or other providers are governed by those services.

3. Optional rewarded advertisements

OctiSculpt uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded advertisements for certain features. Google's advertising technology may collect or process information such as IP address, device identifiers where permitted, advertising identifiers where available and authorized, app interaction and ad impression information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostics. The precise categories depend on your device, region, permissions, Google's SDK behavior, and consent settings.

Advertising information may be used by Google and its partners to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and, where permitted, personalize ads. EvaMariaDreams does not receive your payment-card details through AdMob. For Google's disclosures, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

4. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, OctiSculpt presents a consent or privacy-choice interface through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may be able to review or change advertising choices through the App's Settings → Privacy & Permissions section when privacy options are available for your region. The availability of a privacy-options control may vary by jurisdiction and consent status.

On Apple devices, you can also control permission for app tracking in system privacy settings. Refusing optional consent or tracking permission should not prevent access to the App's core sculpting functions. Rewarded advertisements themselves may be unavailable in some circumstances. Consent choices do not prevent processing that is otherwise permitted or required by law, such as certain security or fraud-prevention activities.

5. Apple purchases and subscriptions

Premium purchases and subscription management are processed by Apple using StoreKit and the App Store. Apple may process account, payment, transaction, and subscription information under its privacy policy. OctiSculpt receives subscription entitlement or transaction status necessary to provide and restore Premium access. EvaMariaDreams does not collect your full payment-card number through the App. See Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Third-party services

OctiSculpt integrates Apple services for purchases and Google services for optional advertising and applicable consent management. These providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country. Their respective privacy policies explain their handling of data, recipients, retention, and international transfers. We do not sell your sculpting projects to advertisers.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation or similar laws apply, processing may be based on: providing requested App functionality and subscription access; legitimate interests such as preventing fraud, maintaining service security, and responding to support requests where permitted; consent for advertising or tracking activities that require it; and compliance with legal obligations. Google and Apple determine the legal bases for processing for which they act as independent controllers.

8. Retention and international transfers

Projects and preferences stored locally remain subject to your device storage, deletion, and backup settings. You can remove local App data by deleting the App, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Google and Apple set retention periods for data they independently process under their own policies. Third-party processing may involve transfers to other countries, with safeguards described by those providers.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, request data portability, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent data-protection authority. To exercise rights concerning information processed by EvaMariaDreams, email privacy@evamariadreams.com. For information held independently by Google or Apple, use their privacy controls and request channels. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

10. Children's privacy

OctiSculpt is a creative tool, not a service intended to solicit personal information from children. Optional advertising may be subject to age-related restrictions and platform rules. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us through support communications, contact us so we can review and address the request.

11. Security

We seek to minimize the personal information processed directly by EvaMariaDreams. No device, network, or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and keep backups of important work.

12. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in OctiSculpt, third-party services, or applicable law. Material changes will be communicated where required. The effective date above identifies the current version.
