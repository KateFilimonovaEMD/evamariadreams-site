Private by design

Profile values and hydration-related inputs are intended to stay on the device. Octi Flow does not rely on third-party analytics or external tracking services.

  • Local data handling.
  • No external analytics services.
  • Separate privacy and terms pages available on the site.

Designed for wellness, not medicine

Octi Flow is a general wellness product focused on routine and habit support. It is not a medical app and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

  • Informational and lifestyle-oriented use.
  • Formulas are based on publicly available sources.
  • Not a substitute for professional medical advice.
