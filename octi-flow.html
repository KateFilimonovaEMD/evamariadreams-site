Octi Flow is intended for general wellness and informational use only. Some features may be available through optional premium access.

What the app is for

Octi Flow is built for users who want a lighter and more consistent way to support hydration-related habits. The app focuses on everyday usability, visual calm, and simple interaction patterns rather than dense tracking workflows.

Routine support

Designed to help users stay more consistent with hydration-related habits and reminders.

Personalization

Includes app settings and experience adjustments that make the product feel more individual.

Calm interface

Built around a clean and approachable visual style that keeps the experience light and clear.
