Support

Help with sculpting, projects, exports, rewarded ads and Premium in OctiSculpt.

Last updated: November 7, 2026
Need help? Email support@evamariadreams.com. Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, OctiSculpt version, and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send private projects unless you choose to.

Getting started

OctiSculpt is a touch-based 3D sculpting workspace for iPad. Start with a primitive, select a sculpting brush and adjust its parameters. Save your project before experimenting with large topology changes.

Projects and backups

Projects are saved locally on the iPad. Export important work and keep backups before deleting the app, resetting your device or installing an older build. Newer project formats may not open in older versions of OctiSculpt.

Importing and exporting

OctiSculpt supports OBJ model import and export. If an imported model fails, try a smaller or simpler OBJ and describe the problem in your support email. When sharing a file, choose a destination you trust and verify the resulting file.

Rewarded ads

Some exports and Photo Mode features may be available after an optional rewarded ad. An internet connection and an available ad may be necessary. If an ad fails or closes before a reward is earned, the feature may remain locked. You can revisit applicable privacy choices in the app settings when the privacy options are available.

Premium purchases

If Premium does not activate after purchase, first confirm the subscription in your Apple Account, then use the app's Restore Purchases option. If it still fails, contact support with the app version and a description of the problem. Never email payment card details or Apple Account passwords.
