Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled in connection with the Octi Flow app and the EvaMariaDreams website.

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Overview

EvaMariaDreams respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled in connection with the Octi Flow app and the EvaMariaDreams website.

Accounts

Octi Flow does not create user accounts at this time.

Information you provide in the app

Octi Flow may allow you to enter information such as age, weight, hydration-related preferences, and other app settings used to personalize your experience.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to EvaMariaDreams.

Local processing

Hydration-related calculations, reminders, and personalization features in Octi Flow are processed locally on your device. EvaMariaDreams does not receive this information through the app.

Notifications

Notifications and reminders, if enabled, are scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases and premium features

Octi Flow may offer premium features through in-app purchases. Premium access may include expanded functionality and visual customization, including themes, backgrounds, and companion variations.

Purchase transactions are processed by the platform provider through which the purchase was made and not directly by EvaMariaDreams.

Analytics and third-party services

Octi Flow does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services at this time.

EvaMariaDreams does not sell personal information and does not share app profile data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Health data and integrations

Octi Flow does not collect data from Apple Health or HealthKit and does not use third-party health-data integrations at this time.

Support communications

If you contact EvaMariaDreams by email, the information you provide in your message, including your email address and any details you choose to share, may be used to respond to your request and provide support.

Data retention

Information entered into the app remains on your device unless you remove it, delete the app, or reset your device settings. Support and privacy emails may be retained for a reasonable period for communication, recordkeeping, and support purposes.

Children’s privacy

Octi Flow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through external servers or third-party services. If you believe personal information has been provided to us directly by email in error, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact EvaMariaDreams at privacy@evamariadreams.com.
