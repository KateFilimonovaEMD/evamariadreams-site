Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy describes how EvaMariaDreams may collect, use, and protect information in connection with Octi Flow and related studio services.

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Overview

EvaMariaDreams is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and what choices may be available to users of Octi Flow and related services.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect limited categories of information such as:

  • Contact information, such as your email address, when you contact us for support or studio inquiries.
  • App usage information, such as feature interactions, session activity, or general engagement data.
  • Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language, and diagnostic information.
  • Purchase-related status information, if subscriptions or in-app purchases are offered in the future.

How information may be used

  • To operate, maintain, and improve Octi Flow and related services.
  • To respond to support requests, questions, and feedback.
  • To understand app performance, reliability, and feature usage.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the security and integrity of the service.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary, including with service providers that support app functionality, analytics, communications, hosting, payments, or legal compliance.

Data retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including support, security, legal compliance, and service improvement.

Your choices

  • You may contact us to request information about your data.
  • You may request correction or deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • You may manage permissions and certain privacy settings through your device or platform settings.

Children’s privacy

Octi Flow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact EvaMariaDreams through the contact information provided on the website.
